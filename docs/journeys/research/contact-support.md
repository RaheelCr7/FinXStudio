## Rules in plain English
Show who is open now. Never ask for passwords or codes. Public messages must not carry account numbers.

## Decisions for BankWorkx®
Three ways with open or closed chips. Chat and message take a topic and text; call has a Call us button and no real number. Signed-in secure messages offered when that journey exists.
